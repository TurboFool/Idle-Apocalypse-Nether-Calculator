HOW TO USE & UNDERSTAND

1. Modifiers & Creature Levels

Configure your Nether Pie level (0–3), toggle Bounty (+1 drop yield), and select your Creature Upgrade Levels (1–3 for Netherling & Demon, 1–2 for Mountain). Creature levels sync bidirectionally with checklist goals and dynamically update drop yields.

2. Live Creature Summons & Undo

Under each creature card in Creature Summons & Yields:

+ (Summon)

Deducts Cost & Adds Drop Yield

- (Undo)

Refunds Cost & Deducts Drop Yield

Hover or tap either button to preview the exact materials deducted or refunded based on your active modifiers.

3. On-Hand Inventory

Enter your current inventory of Orbs, Flames, Crystals, and Stars using the -10, -1, +1, +10 quick buttons or direct text input.

4. Quick Target

Pinned directly above the checklist (collapsed by default). Use it for immediate spur-of-the-moment calculations without creating a permanent goal:

  • Enter any combination of Resources and/or Creature Summons.
  • Click Target to toggle it into your live calculation.
  • Click Clear to zero out all fields.
  • Click Save as Goal... to convert your quick target into a permanent saved goal (automatically untargets and clears Quick Target so costs aren't doubled).

5. Target Checklist & Custom Goals

Select Target on any goals (Creatures, Scrolls, DDD, Lesley, or Custom) to calculate required summons. Multiple targets sum together additively.

  • + Add Custom Goal: Create reusable goals with custom resource and creature targets under the Custom category tab.
  • Edit & Delete: Use the pencil and trash icons on custom goal cards to modify or delete them.
  • Achieved: Mark goals complete to clear their target. Creature level upgrades automatically increase base drop rates!

6. Cascading Summon Math Engine

Solves deficits from highest creature tier to lowest, ensuring your final inventory satisfies all requested resources while honoring creature summon targets:

  • ⭐ Stars deficit (or Mountain requirement) → Summons Mountains (costs Crystals).
  • 💎 Crystals deficit (incl. Mountain costs / Demon requirement) → Summons Demons (costs Flames & Orbs).
  • 🔥 Flames deficit (incl. Demon costs / Netherling requirement) → Summons Netherlings (costs Orbs).
  • 🔮 Orbs deficit (goals + Demon & Netherling summons) → Final Orbs needed.

7. Backup & Reset

Use Export and Import in the header to backup or transfer your progress between devices. Reset clears inventory, active targets, and checklist achievements while safely preserving your library of custom goals.
