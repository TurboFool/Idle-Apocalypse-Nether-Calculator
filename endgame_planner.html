HOW TO USE & UNDERSTAND

1. Modifiers & Creature Levels

Configure your Nether Pie level (0–3), toggle Bounty (+1 drop yield), select your Creature Upgrade Levels (1–3 for Netherling & Demon, 1–2 for Mountain), and toggle each creature's Shiny Skin (+3 drop yield). Creature levels sync bidirectionally with checklist goals and dynamically update drop yields.

2. Live Creature Summons & Undo

Under each creature card in Creature Summons & Yields:

+ (Summon)

Deducts Cost & Adds Drop Yield

- (Undo)

Refunds Cost & Deducts Drop Yield

Hover or tap either button to preview the exact materials deducted or refunded based on your active modifiers.

3. On-Hand Inventory

Enter your current inventory of Orbs, Flames, Crystals, and Stars using the -10, -1, +1, +10 quick buttons or direct text input.

4. Quick Target

Pinned directly above the checklist (collapsed by default). Use it for immediate spur-of-the-moment calculations without creating a permanent goal:

  • Enter any combination of Resources and/or Creature Summons.
  • Track Summon Progress: When enabled, creature summons update progress in real-time with card steppers, decrementing remaining deficits and providing a completion confirmation when finished.
  • Complementary Goal: Mark creature summons as complementary (surplus drops not consumed on completion).
  • Click Target to toggle it into your live calculation.
  • Click Clear to zero out all fields and reset progress.
  • Click Save as Goal... to convert your quick target into a permanent saved goal (automatically passes progress tracking and complementary settings, and untargets Quick Target so costs aren't doubled).

5. Target Checklist & Custom Goals

Select Target on any goals (Creatures, Scrolls, DDD, Lesley, or Custom) to calculate required summons. Multiple targets sum together additively.

  • + Add Custom Goal: Create reusable goals with custom resource and creature targets under the Custom category tab.
  • Live Summon Progress Tracking: When enabled on custom goals, clicking + / - on the main creature summon buttons automatically updates creature progress, preventing resource requirement spikes as you summon. You can also adjust progress manually using the steppers on each card.
  • Completion Confirmation & Auto-Reset: When the final summon completes a tracked goal, a confirmation prompt allows you to confirm or cancel before marking achieved and resetting progress for future runs.
  • Complementary Goals: Mark goals whose creature drops are surplus byproducts (e.g. summoning Mountains for damage) rather than consumed materials.
  • Auto-deduct on achieve: Optional global toggle in the checklist toolbar that automatically subtracts goal costs from on-hand resources when marked as achieved (and refunds them if unchecked).
  • Edit & Delete: Use the pencil and trash icons on custom goal cards to modify or delete them.

6. Action Plan, Roadmap & Cascading Math

The calculator evaluates your inventory and targets from highest creature tier to lowest, providing an actionable roadmap:

  • Action Status: Instantly highlights whether your goal is achievable or displays the exact remaining Nether Orbs you still need.
  • Step-by-Step Roadmap: Outlines the sequential summon chain (Netherlings → Demons → Mountains → Claim Goals) to reach your targets.
  • Detailed Math Breakdown: Expandable technical log explaining the exact arithmetic for each tier.

7. Backup & Reset

Use Export and Import in the header to backup or transfer your progress between devices. Reset clears inventory, active targets, and checklist achievements while safely preserving your library of custom goals.

1. Doomsday Device (DDD) & Multiplier

Enter your current DDD Count. Your Tear Trade multiplier is automatically min(DDD, 10) (up to x10). When you activate the DDD for a new run, click Activate DDD (New Run) to increment your DDD count, clear Tear Trades, and optionally reset Nether Calculator run-specific state while keeping permanent upgrades.

2. Earn: Tear Trades

Plan or mark Done across the 7 sequential trade chains (167 base Tears). Planning or completing a higher tier automatically cascades earlier tiers. Completing trades adds Base Tears × Multiplier to your Tears on Hand. Nether Trades (12 tiers) sync bidirectionally with the Lesley goals in the Nether Calculator.

3. Spend: Pies, Casseroles & Shiny Skins

Browse all 21 Pies & Casseroles and 27 Shiny Skins. Pie levels display a # Shinies badge indicating whether your Owned (green), Planned (blue), or unowned (amber) Shiny Skins meet the requirement. With Auto-deduct Tears on Purchase enabled, marking items Owned deducts their Tear cost from Tears on Hand.

4. Cross-Tool Sync

Nether Pie (levels 0–3), Nether Shiny Skins (Netherling, Demon, Mountain), and the 12 Nether Trades stay synchronized automatically between Lesley's Place and the Nether Calculator.
